A shift and pay tracker for UK hourly workers. It clocks you in when you arrive, works out your pay and take-home, and checks your payslip against the hours you really worked.

  • Clocks you in by location or your work Wi-Fi

How I build

From a Figma frame to a working product.

I’m a product designer first. I also build, using AI-assisted development to turn designs into real, working software fast, then reviewing and refining the code myself.

  1. Design

    Research and design

    User flows, wireframes and high-fidelity UI in Figma, grounded in what people actually need.

  2. Develop

    Real apps, not just prototypes

    Working iPhone, Android and web apps, built in SwiftUI and React Native from the design, with real screens and real data.

  3. Refine

    AI-assisted development

    I build with AI coding tools, then review the code and check every change against the design and the brief.

  4. Launch

    Take it to users

    At dotXline I take products from idea to launch-ready apps. For teams, I hand over clean code or clean specs.

This portfolio is an example: designed and built by me with AI-assisted development, including the particle logo, page transitions and every animation.
