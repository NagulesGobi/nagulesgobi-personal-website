My apps · dotXline LTD

Designed by me. Built by me.

Xpense, Tockbee and FreshBit are the apps of dotXline LTD, my product company in Cardiff. I designed every screen and built all three. Two are live on the App Store, one is in testing.

01iPhone app · dotXline

Xpense

Every penny, in plain sight. A calm, native iPhone expense tracker. Log spending, set budgets and goals, and split costs with friends.

  • iPhone
  • SwiftUI
  • No ads
  • Live on the App Store
Read the case study

02iPhone and Android app · dotXline

Tockbee

Every hour you work, on the record. Tockbee clocks you in when you arrive at work, works out your pay and take-home, and checks your payslip against the hours you really worked.

  • iPhone and Android
  • React Native
  • No tracking
  • Live on the App Store
Read the case study

03iPhone, iPad and Mac app · dotXline

FreshBit

News that checks first. Confirmed news for designers and developers. FreshBit reads 51 trusted sources, groups the articles about the same event and scores each story out of 100.

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac
  • Expo and SwiftUI
  • No ads
  • In TestFlight testing
