Fresh groceries, ordered from home.

greenlifeGrocery shopping app · UX UI design

Overview

Fast, convenient and fresh.

The idea was an app for fast and convenient grocery shopping. The project covered the user flow, wireframes, the visual system and the full set of screens, from sign-in to a placed order.

Design focus

  • Recipes built inSign-up asks for recipe preferences, and one tap adds every ingredient for a recipe to the cart.
  • Browse your wayShop by category, popular items or special offers, or go straight to search.
  • Two paths to checkoutSearch and select, or use Quick shop: both meet at address, payment and order.
  • A fresh, green identityA leaf mark, a green palette (#09B44D, #7DAF25) with slate greys, and Acumin type.
