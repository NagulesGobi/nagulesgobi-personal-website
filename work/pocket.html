Your wallet, in your pocket.

    Overview

    Every expense in a safe place.

    Pocket is an iOS application for keeping a personal wallet. The flow starts with a clear welcome and login, because it is crucial to make the right first impression and to be sure your personal wallet is in a safe place.

    Design focus

    • Welcome, login and sign-upShort, familiar forms with the Pocket mark front and centre.
    • Statistics that compareExtended statistics of your expenses for any period, compared with your average for that period.
    • Balance at a glanceAdd expenses in categories from food to travel, then edit or delete them when you need to.
    • A calm, clean identityA cyan and blue palette (#00B4D8, #0078B7), Circular Std type and a wallet-shaped app icon.
