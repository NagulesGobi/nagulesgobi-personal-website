Every hour you work, on the record.

Tockbee keeps your own record of every hour you work, so you can check your payslip and sort out missing pay.

tockbeeEvery hour you work, on the record.

The problem

Every hour you work should show up on your payslip.

Tockbee keeps an honest record on your phone and nobody else’s, so checking your pay takes a minute instead of an argument.

How it works

  • Arrive. You’re clocked in.By location at a work site or by joining your work Wi-Fi. Every automatic clock-in sends a notification with Undo.
  • Every minute countsOvertime, weekend and night rates and unpaid breaks, worked out as you go.
  • Check it against your payslipCopy in what your payslip says and Tockbee shows any hours or pay that went missing.
  • Your timesheet, ready to shareA PDF or CSV with every shift and how it was recorded.

Text on Tangerine, Sunshine or Coral is always Cocoa. Coral only ever means “on shift right now”. No bees, anywhere.

Honest by design

Tax is an estimate, and the app says so.

Tockbee calls it estimated take-home: after PAYE (including Scottish rates), National Insurance, student loan and pension. Your payslip stays the official figure. And Tockbee collects no data at all.
