Case study · App design · Health

Every puff, counted.

Puff Count is a mobile app that tracks nicotine consumption, lets people set daily limits and helps them work towards quitting vaping.

Role
UX/UI design and branding
Platform
iPhone app
Tools
Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop
Project
Behance ↗
Puff CountTrack nicotine · Unlimited puffs · Daily limits

Chapters

    Overview

    Track it first. Then cut it down.

    Puff Count tracks nicotine consumption, offers unlimited puff logging and lets people set daily limits. Onboarding asks a few short questions, one per screen, so the app starts from the person’s own goal.

    Design focus

    • One question per screenName, goal, the day you quit smoking, reminders and weekly spend on vaping, each on its own calm screen.
    • Goals firstPeople choose “I want to quit vaping” or “Just curious”, so the app meets them where they are.
    • A clear upgradePuff Count Unlimited is offered monthly or yearly with a free trial, and no ads.
    • A bright, friendly identityA bold blue palette (#1058FF, #1497FC), Poppins type and a simple P-mark app icon.
