Every penny, in plain sight.

XpenseEvery penny, in plain sight.

The idea

Spending isn’t the problem. Not knowing is.

Xpense keeps every penny in plain sight, celebrates the months you keep a little more, and never makes you feel bad about the rest.

Design decisions

  • Logged in one tapQuick Add tiles open a new expense with the category already chosen. Your tiles, in your order.
  • Progress, never shameSpend less than last month and Xpense says so: “Nice, £12 less than September.” Spend more and it stays neutral.
  • Numbers firstBuilt natively for iPhone with the calm design of iOS 26. Nothing in the way of the figure you came for.
  • Private by defaultNo ads, no selling data, and receipt photos stay on your iPhone.

The icon

“Less than last month.” Two rounded bars: last month on top, faint; this month below, shorter; and the amber between them is what you kept. Three layers, so iOS gives it Liquid Glass depth.

Principles

  • ClearWe say the number. “£3.40 logged to Food.” Plain, specific, no guessing.
  • CalmQuiet interfaces that stay out of your way and respect your attention.
  • PrivateNo ads. No tracking you across other companies’ apps.
  • Built in-houseDesigned and built inside my own company, with every detail owned end to end.
