Advertising that starts with a conversation.

An AI-powered advertising platform that turns conversations into conversion tools, with a customer support chat app that makes business enquiries easier.

Overview

One chat, the whole campaign.

Chatvertiser is a customer support chatting application for any business. The design focused on a fluid, chat-based interface that simplifies campaign creation, audience targeting and performance tracking, all through one conversational flow.

Design focus

  • Campaigns by chatCreating a campaign feels like describing it, not filling in a long form.
  • A full support deskProfiles, flows, broadcasts, segments, reports, integrations and agents in one dashboard.
  • A clear visual systemA violet palette (#805AD5, #7678EC, #3C348A), one typeface, a consistent icon set and reusable elements.
  • A mark to matchI also designed the Chatvertiser logo, a speech bubble with a pulse.
