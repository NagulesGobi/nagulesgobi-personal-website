Every hour you work, on the record. Tockbee clocks you in when you arrive at work, works out your pay and take-home, and checks your payslip against the hours you really worked.

News that checks first. Confirmed news for designers and developers. FreshBit reads 51 trusted sources, groups the articles about the same event and scores each story out of 100.

How we work

Three habits, in every app.

Clear.

We say the number.

“£3.40 logged to Food.” Plain words, specific amounts, no guessing.

Calm.

Quiet interfaces

They stay out of your way and respect your attention. No guilt, no alarms.

Private.

As little as possible.

No ads. No selling your data. Xpense keeps receipt photos on your iPhone and Tockbee collects no data at all.
