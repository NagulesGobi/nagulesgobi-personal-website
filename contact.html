FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can you guarantee improvements in conversions?+

No designer can honestly guarantee a number. What I can promise is a process built on research, clear goals and testing, so every design decision is aimed at making your product easier to use and easier to say yes to.

How much does it cost to design one web page?+

It depends on scope: the content, the number of states and breakpoints, and whether you need a design system behind it. Tell me about your project and I’ll send a clear quote.

What’s your process like, and how long does it take?+

Discovery and research, then wireframes, UI design, an interactive prototype and a clean handoff to development. Timelines depend on the size of the project, and I’ll share one after our first call.

Can you build what you design?+

Yes. Alongside design, I build with code and AI-assisted development: interactive prototypes, front ends and full apps like Xpense and Tockbee at dotXline. For larger products I work with your engineers and hand over clean specs.

Are you open to full-time roles?+

Yes. I’m open to product design roles as well as freelance projects. Send me the details and let’s talk.
