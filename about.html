My story

Hi, I’m Nagules.

I’m a product designer in the United Kingdom working across UX/UI, web and app design, and brand identity. Over four years I’ve designed 30+ projects across 3 countries, from ad-tech platforms and health apps to shopping apps and logos.

I’m also Director of dotXline LTD, a product company in Cardiff. There I design and build Xpense, a calm iPhone expense tracker, and Tockbee, a shift and pay tracker for UK workers. Owning products end to end taught me to design for the people using them and for the team that has to build them.

I studied for a BSc in Computing, and today I also build what I design. I use AI-assisted development to turn designs into working products quickly, then review and refine the code myself. It means I can prototype in code, test ideas with real users sooner, and talk to engineering teams in their own language.

What went wrong, and how I fixed it

I test my own work until it’s right.

Real problems from Xpense, Tockbee and FreshBit, the apps I design and build at dotXline, and what I changed. I review recordings of real use on an iPhone and fix things build by build: FreshBit alone went through 11 TestFlight builds.

  1. 01User experienceXpense

    The budget that never saved.

    What went wrong

    The monthly budget used the decimal keypad, which has no Done key, and leaving Settings didn’t end the edit. The limit people typed was never saved.

    How I made it right

    The budget now saves as you type, so there’s nothing to confirm and nothing to lose.

  2. 02User experienceTockbee

    Face ID at the wrong moment.

    What went wrong

    Location events open Tockbee in the background, unseen, and every one of those launches asked for Face ID.

    How I made it right

    Face ID now asks only when you open the app yourself.

  3. 03User experienceXpense

    Sign-in codes that didn’t fit.

    What went wrong

    Email sign-in only took codes of one length, so a longer code from the email couldn’t be entered.

    How I made it right

    It now accepts codes of 6 to 8 digits.

  4. 04User interfaceFreshBit

    Lime text you could hardly read.

    Today1.2:1Today6.5:1

    What went wrong

    The selected tab on the light tab bar was lime on a light background: about 1.2:1.

    How I made it right

    Lime became a fill only, always with black text, and electric violet carries text on light backgrounds, 6.5:1. My rule since: never lime for text on a light background.

  5. 05User interfaceFreshBit

    A header that felt custom, not native.

    Custom bounceNative morph

    What went wrong

    The folding header used a custom bouncing animation I had made.

    How I made it right

    I replaced it with Apple’s Liquid Glass morph, so it feels like part of the system.

  6. 06User interfaceXpense

    Custom glass effects on every screen.

    What went wrong

    Xpense had its own glare and reflection effects layered across the interface.

    How I made it right

    I removed them, rebuilt the forms as native SwiftUI, kept glass to the controls only, and respected Reduce Transparency.

  7. 07User interfaceXpense

    A blank flash at launch.

    What went wrong

    Opening the app flashed a blank screen while it checked who was signed in.

    How I made it right

    A branded splash, matching the app icon in light and dark, now stays up until sign-in is known.

  8. 08ProductTockbee

    Asking before every clock-in.

    What went wrong

    Arriving at work by location or Wi-Fi brought a question before Tockbee would clock you in.

    How I made it right

    It now clocks you in and out automatically and sends a notification with Undo: fast by default, easy to reverse.

  9. 09ProductTockbee

    Hours that went missing.

    What went wrong

    Some genuine departures were dropped as repeats, and an old Undo could block the next day. For a pay tracker, a missed shift is missed money.

    How I made it right

    Departures are no longer dropped, an old Undo no longer blocks the next day, sites are watched at 200 m or more, and the app checks your position when it opens to catch anything missed.

How I work

From first question to working product.

The same five steps on every project. Timelines depend on the size of the work, and I share one after our first call.

  1. 01Discovery

    Research the people, the goal and the constraints.

  2. 02Wireframes

    Structure and flow before any polish.

  3. 03UI design

    High-fidelity screens and a visual system.

  4. 04Prototype

    An interactive version to test and refine.

  5. 05Build

    I build it with AI-assisted development, or hand clean specs to your developers.
